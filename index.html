Toward Part-Aware Choral Transcription with Singing Voice Assignment

Hanyu Meng1 · Zhanhong He2 · Zixun Guo3 · Yaolong Ju4

1UNSW · 2UWA · 3Queen Mary University of London · 4Great Bay University

PawCT jointly transcribes soprano, alto, tenor, and bass note events from a single mixed choral recording. The model integrates voice assignment into acoustic transcription instead of treating it as a separate post-processing step.

Paper (PDF) Implementation Demo source

Overview

From one mixture to separate SATB tracks

Conventional choral AMT returns one merged note track. PawCT instead identifies the active vocal parts and directly produces separate note-level soprano, alto, tenor, and bass tracks.

Abstract

Automatic music transcription has advanced for single instruments, but choral systems usually collapse all voices into one track. PawCT is an end-to-end framework for note-level SATB transcription that combines part-specific prediction, part-presence gating, pitch-range priors, ordered continuity, and union-level supervision. On YouChorale, PagCT improves part-agnostic note F1 from 0.237 to 0.382, while PawCT-OC reaches 0.225 mean part-aware note F1.

Method

Joint transcription and voice assignment

A shared CRNN encoder models the mixed recording. Four voice-specific heads estimate note activity for the SATB parts, while union supervision anchors those predictions to the complete note content of the mixture. RP and OC add range-aware and ordering-aware constraints to the same model.

PagCT
Part-agnostic transcription of the mixed choir.
Post-VA
Voice assignment applied after acoustic transcription.
PawCT
Part-aware transcription learned jointly from the audio.

Results

Transcription results on YouChorale

PagCT improves part-agnostic note transcription, while PawCT-OC gives the strongest mean SATB note F1 among the evaluated part-aware systems and transfers better than the two-stage baseline. The complete manuscript tables are reproduced below.
