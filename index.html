Toward Part-Aware Choral Transcription with Singing Voice Assignment

Hanyu Meng · Multimodal Music Research Lab

PawCT jointly transcribes soprano, alto, tenor, and bass note events from a single mixed choral recording. The model integrates voice assignment into acoustic transcription instead of treating it as a separate post-processing step.

Implementation Demo source Reproducibility notes

Method

Joint transcription and voice assignment

A shared CRNN encoder models the mixed recording. Four voice-specific heads estimate note activity for the SATB parts, while union supervision anchors those predictions to the complete note content of the mixture. RP and OC add range-aware and ordering-aware constraints to the same model.

Results

Reported SATB transcription performance

PawCT-OC gives the highest mean SATB note F1 in the comparison below. RP improves frame-level tracking, while OC provides the strongest note-level result.

Mean performance across soprano, alto, tenor, and bass on the manuscript's YouChorale test split.
